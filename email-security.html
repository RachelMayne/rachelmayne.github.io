Email Security Investigation

This was one of those issues that started with something that didn't look quite right and turned into a much more important investigation.

While looking into unusual email activity, I noticed behaviour that suggested our email system could potentially be being misused. I started investigating to understand what was happening and whether there was any wider impact.

At a Glance

Area Email infrastructure
Issue Potential email abuse
Risk Possible disruption to outgoing email
Investigation Log analysis, testing and configuration review.

What I Found

I traced the unusual activity through the email workflow and looked at the available logs and configuration to establish whether it was a one-off event or part of a larger problem.

The investigation showed that the behaviour had the potential to continue if it wasn't addressed.

Rather than treating it simply as an email delivery problem, I looked at the wider implications for the service and the third-party email provider we rely on.

Why It Mattered

The biggest concern wasn't just the suspicious activity itself. Continued abuse could have resulted in our email provider taking action against the account.

Potential impact

If the issue had continued and the account had been suspended, users could have lost the ability to send emails through the platform.

That made it important to deal with the underlying problem rather than simply monitoring it and hoping it stopped.

What I Did

  • Investigated the unusual email activity.
  • Reviewed available evidence and system behaviour.
  • Identified the source of the problem.
  • Worked through the appropriate configuration and security changes.
  • Verified that the unwanted behaviour had stopped.
  • Kept the relevant people informed while the issue was being resolved.

Outcome

The issue was contained before it could develop into a larger email delivery problem.

Afterwards, I was told that resolving it had potentially saved the company a significant amount of stress. If the behaviour had continued, there was a real possibility that our email provider could have suspended the account and affected email sending across the platform.

It was a good reminder that sometimes the most important problems aren't necessarily the ones that arrive as obvious support tickets.

What I Took From It

The main thing I took from this was the importance of paying attention when something doesn't quite add up. A small anomaly can sometimes be a symptom of a much bigger problem.

It also pushed me to learn more about email security and the relationship between application behaviour, infrastructure and third-party services.
