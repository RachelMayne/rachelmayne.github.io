About Me

I'm a Technical Support Engineer with more than 12 years' experience supporting SaaS platforms, digital products, websites and customer-facing technology.

Today I specialise in investigating production issues, reproducing software defects and providing structured technical evidence that enables Product and Engineering teams to identify root causes and improve software reliability.

I enjoy understanding how systems work, identifying patterns within customer-reported issues and combining technical investigation with clear communication to improve both customer experience and product quality.

Collaboration

My Approach

I believe effective Support Engineering is about far more than resolving tickets. It starts with understanding the customer's experience, reproducing the reported behaviour, gathering reliable evidence and communicating findings clearly enough for Engineering teams to investigate efficiently.

Whether investigating booking workflows, payment integrations or third-party services, I enjoy breaking complex problems into smaller, testable scenarios until a repeatable pattern emerges.
