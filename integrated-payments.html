Stripe Payment Investigation

This investigation demonstrates my approach to analysing payment-related issues within a live SaaS platform. The objective was to determine why customer payments were not completing as expected, gather technical evidence and provide Engineering with sufficient information to investigate and resolve the behaviour.

Investigation Summary

System Stripe Payment Integration
Issue Type Payment Workflow Investigation
Severity High
Customer Impact Customers experienced failed or incomplete payment journeys.
Investigation Support Engineering
Outcome Evidence enabled Engineering to investigate the payment workflow and implement improvements.

Background

Customers reported inconsistent payment behaviour during online transactions. Some payments appeared unsuccessful despite following the normal booking process, while others completed successfully under similar conditions.

As payment functionality directly affects customer confidence and business operations, identifying reliable reproduction steps and gathering technical evidence was essential.

Objective

Determine the conditions that caused inconsistent payment behaviour, establish repeatable reproduction steps and provide Engineering with sufficient technical evidence to support investigation.

Investigation Timeline

  1. Reviewed the customer report and payment history.
  2. Recreated the customer's payment workflow.
  3. Tested multiple payment scenarios.
  4. Compared successful and unsuccessful payment attempts.
  5. Reviewed system behaviour throughout the payment process.
  6. Collected technical evidence and supporting screenshots.
  7. Documented findings within Jira.
  8. Worked with Engineering until the behaviour was fully understood.

Investigation

The investigation focused on reproducing the customer journey while systematically isolating each stage of the payment workflow.

  • Recreated the customer's booking and payment configuration.
  • Tested multiple payment scenarios.
  • Compared successful and failed transactions.
  • Reviewed payment workflow behaviour from initiation through completion.
  • Validated expected versus actual outcomes.
  • Repeated testing until reliable reproduction steps were established.

Evidence Collected

Testing identified a repeatable payment workflow issue that occurred under specific conditions. Structured testing isolated the behaviour and enabled detailed documentation for Engineering.

Evidence collected included:

  • Step-by-step reproduction instructions.
  • Expected versus actual payment behaviour.
  • Screenshots demonstrating the workflow.
  • Customer configuration details.
  • Assessment of customer impact.
  • Technical notes for Engineering.

Technologies Used

Healthcare Practice Management SaaS Stripe Jira Raygun Manual Testing Browser Developer Tools

Outcome

Providing structured evidence enabled Engineering to understand the issue quickly, reducing investigation time and supporting improvements to the payment workflow.

Although I was not responsible for implementing the software changes, providing accurate technical documentation improved collaboration between Support, Product and Engineering.

Skills Demonstrated

  • Technical troubleshooting.
  • Payment workflow investigation.
  • Structured bug reproduction.
  • Customer issue ownership.
  • Technical documentation.
  • Cross-functional collaboration.

Technical Reflection

Payment issues are often influenced by configuration, workflow timing and user behaviour. This investigation reinforced the importance of reproducing customer journeys exactly as reported and documenting each stage clearly so Engineering could investigate efficiently.

Why this investigation matters

This investigation demonstrates my approach to Support Engineering: understanding customer impact, reproducing technical issues, gathering structured evidence and collaborating with Engineering teams to improve product reliability.
