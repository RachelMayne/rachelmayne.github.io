Stripe Invoice Sync Investigation

This investigation demonstrates my approach to diagnosing an intermittent payment synchronisation issue between Stripe and a healthcare SaaS platform, using comparative analysis and structured evidence gathering to support Engineering.

Investigation Summary

SystemStripe Payment Integration
Issue TypeInvoice Synchronisation Failure
SeverityMedium
Customer ImpactSuccessful Stripe payments were not always reflected as paid invoices.
InvestigationSupport Engineering
OutcomeEngineering received comparative evidence to narrow potential causes.

Background

A customer reported that Stripe payments completed successfully, but the corresponding invoices did not automatically update as paid within the platform.

The behaviour appeared inconsistent, suggesting an intermittent synchronisation issue rather than a complete payment outage.

Objective

Determine whether the behaviour matched an existing known issue or represented a separate synchronisation failure, and provide Engineering with sufficient evidence for investigation.

Investigation Timeline

  1. Reviewed the reported payment records.
  2. Compared successful and failed invoice updates.
  3. Checked Stripe transaction behaviour.
  4. Reviewed account management processing.
  5. Compared findings against an existing Online Booking/Stripe investigation.
  6. Collected transaction IDs, timelines and examples.
  7. Escalated with structured technical evidence.

Investigation

  • Analysed affected payment records.
  • Compared working and non-working synchronisation events.
  • Looked for recurring timing and workflow patterns.
  • Differentiated similarities from the known issue while identifying behavioural differences.
  • Documented findings clearly for Engineering.

Evidence Collected

  • Stripe transaction IDs.
  • Invoice synchronisation timelines.
  • Comparisons of successful and failed transactions.
  • Observed behavioural patterns.
  • Internal escalation notes.

Technologies Used

Clinical SaaS Platform Stripe Jira Browser Developer Tools Manual Testing

Outcome

The comparative evidence helped Engineering narrow potential causes and distinguish the issue from an existing Stripe-related investigation.

The investigation also highlighted that the behaviour appeared intermittent rather than indicating a complete service outage, supporting faster technical triage.

Skills Demonstrated

  • Payment integration troubleshooting.
  • Comparative technical analysis.
  • Pattern recognition.
  • Production issue investigation.
  • Cross-functional collaboration.
  • Evidence-based escalation.

Technical Reflection

Comparing successful and failed transactions is often one of the quickest ways to isolate intermittent integration issues. Providing Engineering with structured examples reduced investigation time and improved the quality of technical triage.

Why this investigation matters

This investigation demonstrates my ability to diagnose complex third-party integration issues, distinguish known defects from new behaviours, and provide high-quality evidence that supports efficient root cause analysis.
