How I Like to Work

I tend to get curious when something doesn't make sense.

If a customer reports something that shouldn't be happening, I like to reproduce it, look at the evidence, test different possibilities and work backwards until I can understand what's actually happening.

Sometimes that means browser tools, API responses, logs or third-party documentation. Sometimes it means comparing a successful example with a failed one. And sometimes it means learning something completely new because the problem requires it.
