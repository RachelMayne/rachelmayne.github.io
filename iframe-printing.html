Assessment Printing Investigation

This investigation demonstrates my approach to analysing an intermittent customer-reported printing issue within a live SaaS platform. The objective was to reproduce the reported behaviour, determine the conditions that triggered it and provide Engineering with clear evidence to support further investigation.

Investigation Summary

System Assessment Printing
Issue Type Rendering / Printing Behaviour
Severity Medium
Customer Impact Printed assessment documents displayed unexpected formatting, reducing readability.
Investigation Support Engineering
Outcome Issue reproduced, evidence gathered and documented for Engineering investigation.

Background

A customer reported that printed assessment documents were not formatting correctly. The issue only occurred under certain circumstances and could not initially be reproduced consistently, suggesting an environmental or rendering-related issue rather than a general platform fault.

Because printed assessments are frequently used during clinical workflows, understanding the behaviour was important to ensure customers could reliably generate professional documentation.

Objective

Determine the conditions that caused the formatting inconsistency, establish reliable reproduction steps and provide Engineering with structured technical evidence.

Investigation Timeline

  1. Customer reported inconsistent formatting when printing assessment documents.
  2. Reviewed the customer's assessment configuration and document layout.
  3. Recreated the assessment using equivalent data.
  4. Tested printing through multiple browsers and print methods.
  5. Compared PDF generation with browser printing behaviour.
  6. Repeated testing using different content lengths and assessment templates.
  7. Identified repeatable conditions affecting document rendering.
  8. Documented findings, screenshots and reproduction steps within Jira.

Investigation

The investigation focused on isolating variables that could influence printed output while recreating the customer's workflow as closely as possible.

  • Recreated the customer's assessment using equivalent configuration.
  • Compared browser print previews and exported PDF output.
  • Tested different browsers to eliminate browser-specific behaviour.
  • Varied assessment length and formatting to identify triggering conditions.
  • Captured screenshots showing expected versus actual output.
  • Documented reproducible behaviour for Engineering.

Evidence Collected

Testing established consistent reproduction steps demonstrating that document formatting changed under specific rendering conditions. Structured testing eliminated several potential causes and narrowed the investigation to behaviour affecting document presentation.

Evidence collected included:

  • Step-by-step reproduction instructions.
  • Expected versus actual printed output.
  • Screenshots demonstrating formatting differences.
  • Browser and environment details.
  • Assessment configuration.
  • Technical notes for Engineering.

Technologies Used

WriteUpp Assessment Forms Jira Browser Developer Tools Manual Testing PDF Printing

Outcome

Providing structured reproduction steps and supporting evidence enabled Engineering to investigate the rendering behaviour efficiently without needing to recreate the customer's workflow independently.

Although I was not responsible for implementing any software changes, producing clear technical documentation reduced investigation time and supported effective collaboration between Support, Product and Engineering.

Skills Demonstrated

  • Technical troubleshooting within a live SaaS platform.
  • Structured bug reproduction.
  • Systematic testing and comparison.
  • Technical documentation.
  • Evidence gathering.
  • Cross-functional collaboration.

Technical Reflection

This investigation reinforced the importance of changing one variable at a time during testing. Comparing browser behaviour, document output and customer configuration helped isolate the circumstances under which the issue occurred and produced evidence that Engineering could use immediately.

Why this investigation matters

This investigation demonstrates my approach to Support Engineering: understanding customer impact, reproducing complex software behaviour, gathering structured evidence and communicating findings clearly to Engineering teams to support efficient problem resolution.
