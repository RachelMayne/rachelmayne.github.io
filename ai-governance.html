AI Governance & DPIA Knowledge Leadership

This investigation demonstrates my ability to research complex technical and compliance topics, translate them into clear customer-facing guidance, and collaborate with senior technical stakeholders to ensure accuracy before publication.

Background

Customers evaluating an AI-powered clinical documentation product raised detailed questions relating to governance, Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIAs), implementation and organisational compliance.

Accurate responses required balancing technical understanding with clear customer communication while ensuring alignment with internal policy.

Objective

Produce technically accurate customer-facing guidance covering AI governance and DPIA considerations, validate the content with senior technical leadership and support consistent customer communications.

Investigation Timeline

  1. Reviewed customer questions and recurring themes.
  2. Researched AI governance and implementation considerations.
  3. Drafted comprehensive customer guidance.
  4. Collaborated with senior technical leadership for validation.
  5. Refined responses following feedback.
  6. Shared approved guidance with customers.
  7. Contributed towards future knowledge-base discussions.
