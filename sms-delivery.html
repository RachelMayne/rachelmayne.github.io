SMS Delivery Investigation

This investigation demonstrates my approach to diagnosing SMS delivery failures within a live healthcare SaaS platform. The objective was to determine why appointment messages were not reaching recipients, gather technical evidence and provide Engineering with sufficient information to investigate and resolve the behaviour.

Investigation Summary

SystemSMS Notification Service
Issue TypeMessage Delivery Investigation
SeverityHigh
Customer ImpactPatients did not receive appointment reminders and verification messages.
InvestigationSupport Engineering
OutcomeEvidence enabled Engineering and the SMS provider to identify the root cause.

Background

Customers reported that SMS reminders were not consistently arriving despite appearing as successfully sent within the application.

Because SMS reminders reduce missed appointments and support customer communication, identifying the cause quickly was essential.

Objective

Determine where the SMS workflow was failing, establish repeatable evidence and provide Engineering with detailed technical findings.

Investigation Timeline

  1. Reviewed customer reports and message history.
  2. Verified recipient details and notification settings.
  3. Compared successful and failed SMS deliveries.
  4. Reviewed provider responses and delivery status.
  5. Gathered supporting evidence and screenshots.
  6. Documented findings within Jira.
  7. Worked with Engineering and the third-party SMS provider.
  8. Confirmed resolution following deployment.

Investigation

The investigation focused on tracing the complete notification journey from message generation through to provider delivery responses.

  • Recreated customer notification scenarios.
  • Compared successful and unsuccessful deliveries.
  • Validated recipient configuration.
  • Reviewed provider response codes.
  • Captured expected versus actual behaviour.
  • Produced reliable reproduction evidence.

Evidence Collected

Evidence demonstrated that message generation was successful but delivery behaviour varied under specific conditions, enabling Engineering to continue investigation with the SMS provider.

  • Step-by-step reproduction instructions.
  • Delivery status comparisons.
  • Screenshots and logs.
  • Configuration details.
  • Customer impact assessment.
  • Technical notes for Engineering.

Technologies Used

Clinical SaaS Platform Bandwidth Jira Raygun Manual Testing Browser Developer Tools

Outcome

Providing structured technical evidence reduced investigation time and supported collaboration between Support, Engineering and the third-party messaging provider until the issue was resolved.

Although I was not responsible for implementing the software changes, my investigation enabled faster diagnosis and clearer communication throughout the incident.

Skills Demonstrated

  • Technical troubleshooting.
  • Third-party integration investigation.
  • Structured bug reproduction.
  • Customer issue ownership.
  • Technical documentation.
  • Cross-functional collaboration.

Technical Reflection

Investigating third-party integrations requires validating every stage of the workflow rather than assuming failures originate within the application. Structured evidence accelerated investigation and resolution.

Why this investigation matters

This investigation demonstrates my ability to diagnose complex integration issues, communicate effectively with Engineering and external providers, and produce evidence that improves service reliability.
