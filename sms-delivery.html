SMS Delivery Investigation

This investigation demonstrates my approach to analysing third-party integration issues within a live SaaS platform. The objective was to determine why SMS appointment reminders were consistently failing for a specific group of users, gather technical evidence and identify whether the issue originated within the application or an external provider.

Investigation Summary

System SMS Notifications
Issue Type Third-party Integration Investigation
Severity Medium
Customer Impact Patients were not receiving appointment reminder SMS messages.
Investigation Support Engineering
Outcome Investigation isolated the issue to external carrier support through the SMS provider.

Background

A customer reported that appointment reminder SMS messages were not being delivered to patients using Jersey mobile numbers. SMS delivery was working successfully for UK mainland mobile networks, suggesting the problem was limited to a specific destination.

As SMS reminders are an important part of the patient booking journey, identifying whether the issue originated within the application, SMS provider or mobile network was essential before escalation.

Objective

Determine whether the SMS delivery failures were caused by application behaviour, configuration, message generation or external carrier routing, and provide Engineering with sufficient evidence for escalation.

Investigation Timeline

  1. Customer reported SMS reminders failing for Jersey mobile numbers.
  2. Verified reminder configuration within the customer's account.
  3. Confirmed reminder generation was functioning normally.
  4. Compared successful UK deliveries against unsuccessful Jersey deliveries.
  5. Tested multiple destination numbers.
  6. Reviewed provider documentation and supported destinations.
  7. Confirmed Bandwidth did not currently support SMS delivery to Jersey networks.
  8. Provided findings and documented investigation for Product and Engineering.

Investigation

The investigation focused on eliminating each possible failure point before concluding the issue originated outside the application.

  • Verified SMS reminders were being generated successfully.
  • Confirmed customer configuration was correct.
  • Compared successful and unsuccessful message deliveries.
  • Tested different mobile destinations to identify a geographical pattern.
  • Reviewed third-party provider documentation.
  • Confirmed the behaviour matched current carrier support limitations.
  • Documented findings and recommended escalation path.

Evidence Collected

Testing demonstrated that reminder generation, scheduling and application behaviour were operating correctly. Failures occurred only when messages were sent to Jersey mobile numbers, indicating the issue existed beyond the application itself.

Evidence collected included:

  • Configuration validation.
  • Comparison of successful and unsuccessful deliveries.
  • Destination-specific testing.
  • Provider documentation.
  • Assessment of customer impact.
  • Technical notes supporting Engineering review.

Technologies Used

WriteUpp Bandwidth SMS Notifications Jira Manual Testing Third-party Integrations

Outcome

The investigation demonstrated that the application was functioning correctly and isolated the failure to external carrier support provided through Bandwidth. This prevented unnecessary development work and enabled Product and Engineering to pursue the appropriate escalation route.

Although the underlying limitation was outside the application's control, providing structured evidence ensured Engineering could quickly understand the issue, communicate accurately with stakeholders and manage customer expectations.

Technical Reflection

This investigation reinforced the importance of avoiding assumptions during troubleshooting. By systematically validating each stage of the SMS workflow, I was able to eliminate the application as the source of the problem and identify an external provider limitation. Structured investigation reduced unnecessary engineering effort and ensured escalation was based on evidence rather than speculation.

Why this investigation matters

This investigation demonstrates my approach to Support Engineering: understanding customer impact, systematically eliminating possible causes, investigating third-party integrations, gathering technical evidence and communicating findings that enable informed engineering decisions.
