SMS Delivery Investigation

This investigation demonstrates my approach to diagnosing SMS delivery failures within a live healthcare SaaS platform. The objective was to determine why appointment reminders were not reaching recipients, gather technical evidence and provide Engineering with sufficient information to investigate and resolve the behaviour.

Investigation Summary

SystemSMS Notification Service
Issue TypeMessage Delivery Investigation
SeverityHigh
Customer ImpactPatients did not receive appointment reminders.
InvestigationSupport Engineering
OutcomeEvidence enabled Engineering and the SMS provider to identify the cause.

Background

Customers reported that SMS reminders were not consistently arriving despite appearing as successfully sent within the application.

Because SMS reminders are critical to reducing missed appointments, identifying the cause quickly was essential.

Objective

Determine where the SMS workflow was failing, establish repeatable evidence and provide Engineering with detailed technical findings.

Investigation Timeline

  1. Reviewed customer reports and message history.
  2. Verified recipient details and notification settings.
  3. Compared successful and failed deliveries.
  4. Reviewed provider delivery responses.
  5. Collected supporting evidence.
  6. Documented findings within Jira.
  7. Worked with Engineering and the SMS provider.
  8. Confirmed the resolution.

Investigation

The investigation focused on tracing the notification workflow from message creation through to provider delivery.

  • Recreated customer notification scenarios.
  • Compared successful and failed deliveries.
  • Validated recipient configuration.
  • Reviewed provider response codes.
  • Captured expected versus actual behaviour.
  • Produced reliable reproduction evidence.

Evidence Collected

Testing demonstrated that message generation completed successfully while delivery behaviour varied under specific conditions.

  • Step-by-step reproduction instructions.
  • Delivery status comparisons.
  • Screenshots and logs.
  • Configuration details.
  • Customer impact assessment.
  • Technical notes for Engineering.

Technologies Used

Clinical SaaS Platform Bandwidth Jira Raygun Manual Testing Browser Developer Tools

Outcome

Providing structured evidence enabled faster investigation and effective collaboration between Support, Engineering and the SMS provider.

Although I did not implement the software changes, my investigation reduced diagnosis time and improved communication throughout the incident.

Technical Reflection

This investigation reinforced the importance of validating every stage of an external integration and documenting evidence clearly for Engineering.
