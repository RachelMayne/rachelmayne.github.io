Technical Portfolio

This portfolio brings together a selection of investigations I've worked on within live SaaS environments. They show how I approach production issues, reproduce customer reported behaviour, gather technical evidence and work with Product and Engineering teams to understand what's happening and move investigations forward.

Most of these investigations started with a customer reporting that "something isn't working". My role was to understand the behaviour, reproduce it where possible, identify patterns and provide enough evidence for the right people to take the investigation further.

Why This Portfolio

Rather than simply listing technologies I've used, I wanted to show how I investigate problems in practice. Each case study is based on a real production issue and explains how I approached it, what I tested, what I found and how the investigation supported Product or Engineering teams.

Together they cover APIs, payment integrations, third party services, production incidents, AI governance and customer facing SaaS applications, but more importantly they show the way I think when faced with an unfamiliar technical problem.
