Calendar Synchronisation Investigation

This investigation demonstrates my approach to diagnosing calendar synchronisation issues between a healthcare SaaS platform and external calendar providers. The objective was to identify why appointments were not synchronising correctly, gather technical evidence and provide Engineering with sufficient information to investigate and resolve the behaviour.

Investigation Summary

SystemCalendar Synchronisation
Issue TypeThird-Party Integration Investigation
SeverityHigh
Customer ImpactAppointments were missing or delayed within connected calendars.

OutcomeEvidence enabled Engineering to isolate the synchronisation issue and implement improvements.

Background

Customers reported that appointments created within the platform were not consistently appearing in linked Google Calendar and Microsoft Outlook calendars.

Because clinicians rely on accurate calendar synchronisation, identifying the cause quickly was essential to minimise disruption.

Objective

Determine where the synchronisation workflow failed, establish reliable reproduction steps and provide Engineering with detailed technical evidence.

Investigation Timeline

  1. Reviewed customer reports and account configuration.
  2. Verified calendar connection status.
  3. Recreated synchronisation scenarios.
  4. Compared successful and unsuccessful sync attempts.
  5. Reviewed logs and synchronisation behaviour.
  6. Collected screenshots and supporting evidence.
  7. Documented findings within Jira.
  8. Worked with Engineering until the issue was resolved.

Investigation

The investigation focused on tracing appointments from creation through to synchronisation with external calendar providers.

  • Validated integration settings.
  • Tested appointment creation and updates.
  • Compared successful and failed synchronisation events.
  • Reviewed expected versus actual behaviour.
  • Repeated testing to establish reliable reproduction steps.
  • Provided structured evidence for Engineering.

Evidence Collected

Testing demonstrated that synchronisation failures occurred only under specific conditions, allowing Engineering to reproduce the behaviour consistently.

  • Step-by-step reproduction instructions.
  • Expected versus actual synchronisation behaviour.
  • Screenshots and log evidence.
  • Calendar configuration details.
  • Customer impact assessment.
  • Technical notes for Engineering.

Technologies Used

Clinical SaaS Platform Cronofy Google Calendar Microsoft Outlook Jira Raygun

Outcome

Providing structured technical evidence reduced investigation time and improved collaboration between Support, Engineering and the integration provider.

Although I did not implement the software changes, my investigation enabled Engineering to reproduce the issue and deliver a reliable resolution.

Technical Reflection

This investigation reinforced the importance of understanding API-driven synchronisation workflows and validating each stage independently before identifying the source of failure.
