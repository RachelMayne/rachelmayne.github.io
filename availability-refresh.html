Online Booking Availability Refresh Investigation

This started with a simple question: why wasn't a change to appointment availability showing up in Online Booking?

Customers were changing clinician availability in the main platform, but patients could sometimes continue seeing the old availability when booking online.

At a Glance

System Online Booking
Issue Availability not refreshing
Impact Patients could see outdated appointment availability.
Tools Developer Tools, Jira, Raygun and manual testing.

What Was Happening?

The updated availability was being saved correctly in the main platform, but Online Booking wasn't always reflecting the change.

I tested different appointment types and configurations, compared the two systems and used browser Developer Tools to look at what was happening during the refresh.

The aim was to find a repeatable pattern rather than simply treating it as a timing issue.

The Clue

I found that removing a clinician from Online Booking availability and then adding them again forced the updated availability to appear.

Why that mattered

The workaround suggested that the underlying availability was correct and that something was preventing the Online Booking refresh from being triggered.

Then It Happened Again

Several months later, another customer reported the same behaviour. Because the original investigation had been documented, I was able to compare the two incidents.

The symptoms, reproduction steps and workaround matched, showing that this was a repeatable issue rather than an isolated incident.

What Was Found

Engineering determined that the refresh behaved differently depending on who changed the clinician's working hours.

Changes made by the clinician themselves triggered the Online Booking refresh correctly. Changes made by a site administrator did not trigger the same refresh process.

Confirmed root cause

Administrator changes to clinician working hours were not triggering the Online Booking availability refresh, leaving the previous availability visible until the configuration was manually saved.

Outcome

The investigation, together with the later matching incident, gave Engineering a repeatable scenario to work with and helped identify the condition causing the refresh failure.

The main lesson for me was the value of turning a vague production issue into something reproducible. It also showed why keeping good investigation notes can be useful long after the original issue has been closed.
