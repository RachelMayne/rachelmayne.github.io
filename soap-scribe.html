SOAP Scribe Investigation

This investigation demonstrates my approach to diagnosing an issue affecting an AI-assisted SOAP note scribing feature within a Healthcare Practice Management Platform. The objective was to identify why generated clinical notes were incomplete or failed to save consistently, gather technical evidence and provide Engineering with sufficient information to investigate and resolve the behaviour.

Investigation Summary

SeverityHigh

Background

Customers reported that AI-generated SOAP notes occasionally failed to populate expected sections or did not save correctly after generation.

As the feature formed part of clinicians' documentation workflow, identifying the cause quickly was essential to minimise disruption.

Objective

Determine the conditions that caused inconsistent SOAP note generation, produce reliable reproduction steps and document actionable evidence for Engineering.

Investigation Timeline

  1. Reviewed customer reports and affected records.
  2. Recreated the workflow using test data.
  3. Compared successful and unsuccessful note generation.
  4. Reviewed application responses and error information.
  5. Captured timestamps, screenshots and supporting evidence.
  6. Documented findings in Jira.
  7. Worked with Engineering during investigation.
  8. Verified behaviour following deployment.

Investigation

The investigation focused on the complete workflow from initiating AI note generation through to reviewing and saving the completed SOAP record.

  • Validated feature configuration.
  • Tested multiple clinical scenarios.
  • Compared expected and actual outputs.
  • Reviewed monitoring data and browser behaviour.
  • Established repeatable reproduction steps.
  • Provided structured evidence for Engineering.

Evidence Collected

  • Step-by-step reproduction instructions.
  • Screenshots and timestamps.
  • Application monitoring information.
  • Configuration comparisons.
  • Customer impact assessment.
  • Technical notes for Engineering.

Technologies Used

Healthcare Practice Management Platform AI SOAP Scribe Jira Raygun Browser Developer Tools Manual Testing

Outcome

Providing structured technical evidence enabled Engineering to reproduce the issue consistently, improve feature reliability and reduce disruption to clinicians.

The investigation strengthened collaboration between Support and Engineering while improving future investigations involving AI-assisted workflows.

Skills Demonstrated

  • AI feature troubleshooting.
  • Workflow analysis.
  • Bug reproduction.
  • Technical documentation.
  • Evidence gathering.
  • Cross-functional collaboration.

Technical Reflection

Following the complete workflow and comparing successful and unsuccessful outcomes helped isolate the conditions that affected AI-generated documentation and provided Engineering with reproducible evidence.

Why this investigation matters

This investigation demonstrates my ability to troubleshoot AI-powered SaaS features, analyse complex user workflows and provide high-quality technical evidence that supports efficient production issue resolution.
