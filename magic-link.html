Mobile SMS Magic Link Investigation

This investigation demonstrates my approach to diagnosing an authentication issue affecting SMS magic links within a healthcare SaaS platform. The objective was to determine why some users could not access secure links sent by SMS, gather technical evidence and provide Engineering with sufficient information to investigate and resolve the behaviour.

Investigation Summary

SystemSMS Authentication
Issue TypeMagic Link Investigation
SeverityHigh
Customer ImpactUsers were unable to access secure login links sent by SMS.
InvestigationSupport Engineering

Background

Customers reported that SMS messages were delivered successfully but tapping the magic link either failed to authenticate or redirected users unexpectedly.

Because the feature supported secure access from mobile devices, identifying the cause quickly was essential to minimise disruption.

Objective

Determine where the authentication workflow failed, establish reliable reproduction steps and provide Engineering with structured technical evidence.

Investigation Timeline

  1. Reviewed customer reports.
  2. Verified SMS delivery status.
  3. Tested links across multiple mobile devices and browsers.
  4. Compared successful and failed authentication attempts.
  5. Captured logs and screenshots.
  6. Documented findings within Jira.
  7. Worked with Engineering during investigation.
  8. Confirmed successful resolution.

Investigation

The investigation focused on following the complete authentication journey from SMS delivery through to successful account access.

  • Validated SMS delivery.
  • Tested multiple mobile platforms and browsers.
  • Compared expected and actual authentication behaviour.
  • Reviewed token expiry and redirect behaviour.
  • Repeated testing to establish reliable reproduction steps.
  • Provided structured evidence for Engineering.

Evidence Collected

  • Step-by-step reproduction instructions.
  • Device and browser comparisons.
  • Screenshots.
  • Configuration details.
  • Customer impact assessment.
  • Technical notes for Engineering.

Root Cause Analysis

The investigation confirmed that SMS messages were being delivered successfully, indicating that the messaging service and notification workflow were functioning as expected. Repeated testing demonstrated that the failure occurred after users received the message, during the authentication process initiated by the magic link.

By comparing successful and unsuccessful authentication attempts across multiple mobile devices and browsers, I established that the issue was not related to SMS delivery, recipient configuration or user permissions. The behaviour only occurred under specific conditions after the secure link had been opened, suggesting the fault existed within the application's authentication workflow rather than the third-party messaging provider.

Providing Engineering with repeatable reproduction steps, affected device information, browser comparisons, timestamps and customer examples enabled them to isolate the authentication workflow more efficiently. Structured testing also ruled out common causes such as expired links, failed SMS delivery and incorrect customer configuration.

Although the software fix was implemented by Engineering, the investigation significantly reduced diagnosis time by identifying the precise conditions required to reproduce the authentication failure consistently.

Confirmed Root Cause

SMS messages were generated and delivered successfully. The authentication failure occurred within the application's magic link processing workflow after the user opened the secure link, rather than within the SMS delivery service itself.
