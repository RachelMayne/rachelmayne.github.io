Form Delivery Investigation

This investigation demonstrates my approach to diagnosing a form delivery issue affecting customer communications within a healthcare practice management SaaS platform. The objective was to identify why completed forms were not reaching recipients consistently, gather technical evidence and provide Engineering with sufficient information to investigate and resolve the behaviour.

Background

Customers reported that forms appeared to send successfully but were not always received by patients.

Because the issue affected communication and operational workflows, a structured investigation was required.

Objective

Trace the complete delivery workflow, identify where failures occurred and provide Engineering with reproducible evidence.

Investigation Timeline

  1. Reviewed customer reports.
  2. Verified form configuration.
  3. Tested multiple delivery scenarios.
  4. Compared successful and failed deliveries.
  5. Collected logs, timestamps and screenshots.
  6. Documented findings in Jira.
  7. Worked with Engineering to reproduce the issue.
  8. Verified behaviour after deployment.

Investigation

The investigation followed the end-to-end workflow from form generation through to delivery confirmation.

  • Validated customer configuration.
  • Compared expected and actual delivery behaviour.
  • Reviewed application logs and monitoring data.
  • Tested different recipient scenarios.
  • Created reliable reproduction steps.
  • Provided structured evidence for Engineering.

Evidence Collected

  • Reproduction steps.
  • Delivery timestamps.
  • Screenshots.
  • Configuration comparisons.
  • Customer impact assessment.
  • Technical notes for Engineering.

Root Cause Analysis

The investigation confirmed that forms were being generated successfully and requests were reaching the external delivery API. Testing showed that issues only occurred when a large number of forms were submitted within a short period, causing delivery requests to exceed the rate the API could process reliably.

By comparing successful and unsuccessful delivery attempts, I established that the application workflow and customer configuration were functioning correctly. API responses confirmed that requests sent in rapid succession could be delayed or rejected due to rate limiting, meaning some forms were not delivered even though users initially received confirmation that the send process had started.

Providing Engineering with repeatable reproduction steps, delivery timestamps, API responses and customer examples enabled them to reproduce the behaviour consistently. The investigation ruled out common causes such as incorrect form configuration, recipient details and application permissions, confirming that the issue only occurred during high-volume submission scenarios.

Following the investigation, Engineering updated the customer-facing warning message to make the delivery limitations clearer and enhanced the delivered reports so customers could more accurately identify which forms had been processed successfully.

Confirmed Root Cause

Forms were submitted to the delivery API successfully, but sending large numbers of requests in rapid succession exceeded the API's processing limits. Engineering improved the warning message presented to users and updated the delivered report to provide clearer visibility of successful deliveries.
