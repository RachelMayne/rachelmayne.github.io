Online Booking Edge Case Investigation

This investigation demonstrates my approach to analysing a customer-reported issue within a live healthcare SaaS platform. The objective was to identify a repeatable booking edge case, gather technical evidence and provide Engineering with sufficient information to investigate and resolve the reported behaviour.

Investigation Summary

SystemOnline Booking
Issue TypeBooking Workflow Edge Case
SeverityHigh
Customer ImpactPatients were unable to complete valid online bookings.
InvestigationSupport Engineering
OutcomeEngineering reproduced the defect and released a production fix.

Background

A customer reported that appointments could not always be booked online despite appointment availability appearing to exist. The behaviour was intermittent and suggested an edge case rather than a general platform failure.

As Online Booking is customer-facing, identifying the cause quickly was important to minimise disruption and restore confidence.

Objective

Determine why valid appointments were being rejected, establish reliable reproduction steps and provide Engineering with structured technical evidence.

Investigation Timeline

  1. Reviewed the original report.
  2. Recreated the customer's booking configuration.
  3. Tested multiple booking scenarios.
  4. Compared successful and unsuccessful outcomes.
  5. Repeated testing using different availability combinations.
  6. Identified the interaction between booking restrictions and availability.
  7. Documented findings in Jira.
  8. Engineering reproduced the issue and deployed a production fix.

Investigation

Outcome

Providing structured evidence enabled Engineering to reproduce the defect quickly, prioritise the investigation and implement an out-of-hours production fix.

Clear technical documentation reduced investigation time and supported collaboration between Support, Product and Engineering.
