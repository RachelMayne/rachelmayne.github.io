Filename Truncation Investigation

This investigation demonstrates my approach to diagnosing a document management issue where uploaded filenames were unexpectedly truncated. The objective was to reproduce the behaviour, identify the trigger and provide Engineering with structured technical evidence.

Investigation Summary

SystemDocument Management
Issue TypeFilename Handling Defect
SeverityMedium
Customer ImpactUploaded files displayed incomplete names.
InvestigationSupport Engineering
OutcomeEngineering reproduced the issue and implemented a fix.

Background

Customers reported that long filenames appeared correctly before upload but were shortened after being saved.

Testing focused on identifying when the filename changed during the upload workflow.

Objective

Identify reliable reproduction steps and provide Engineering with detailed technical evidence.

Investigation Timeline

  1. Reviewed customer examples.
  2. Uploaded files with different filename lengths.
  3. Compared expected and actual behaviour.
  4. Captured screenshots.
  5. Documented findings in Jira.
  6. Worked with Engineering until resolved.

Investigation

  • Tested multiple filename lengths.
  • Repeated uploads.
  • Compared browser behaviour.
  • Validated document storage workflow.
  • Produced reliable reproduction steps.

Evidence Collected

  • Reproduction steps.
  • Screenshots.
  • Filename comparisons.
  • Customer impact assessment.
  • Technical notes.
